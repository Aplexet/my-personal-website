My First Blog Post

Published July 29, 2026

Starting a personal website felt like the perfect small project: it gave me room to combine structure, design, and my own personality.

Why I Started

I wanted a place where I could introduce myself and keep track of what I create. Building a website also helped me see how a simple idea becomes something real through many small decisions.

At first, an empty page felt intimidating. Once I divided it into a header, sections, cards, and a footer, the project became much easier to understand.

Engineering Meets Art

HTML gave the page its structure, almost like a frame or blueprint. CSS let me work with color, spacing, shape, and balance. I liked seeing technical rules create a visual result.

The red, black, and white palette keeps the design focused, while geometric details connect the page to both engineering and art.

What I Learned

Here are a few lessons I am taking into my next project:

  • Large projects feel simpler when they are divided into sections.
  • Clear names and comments make code easier to update.
  • Good design should work on both computers and phones.
  • It is okay to improve an idea one version at a time.

Conclusion

This website is only a beginning. I am excited to keep learning, add future projects, and use this blog to document what I discover.
